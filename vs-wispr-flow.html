WhisperWriter vs Wispr Flow

Talk and it types, and your voice stays on your PC.

Wispr Flow is a good dictation app, but it sends your voice to its servers to turn it into text, and the free plan stops at 2,000 words a week. WhisperWriter does the same job on your own Windows PC. It works offline, needs no account, has no word limit, and you pay once, from $3.

Which one is for you

Pick the one that fits how you work

Pick WhisperWriter if

  • you use Windows
  • you do not want your voice or your screen sent to anyone
  • you want to say Hinglish and get English back
  • you would rather pay once than every month
  • you dictate a lot and hit word limits

Pick Wispr Flow if

  • you use a Mac, iPhone or Android phone
  • you speak languages other than English and Hindi
  • you want AI to tidy and reformat your text
  • your PC has no NVIDIA GPU or little disk space
