How it works

Three steps. No window to switch to.

01

Hold F4

Put your cursor anywhere you can type. Hold the F4 key (you can change the key in settings).

02

Speak

Talk normally, in English or Hinglish. A small status bar shows that it is listening.

03

Let go

Your speech is turned into text on your own PC and typed for you, right where the cursor is.

FAQ

Questions

Does it send my voice to the internet?

No. The speech models run on your own computer. Your voice and your text stay on your PC.

Do I need an NVIDIA graphics card?

No, but it helps a lot. With an NVIDIA GPU, text appears in about a second. On the CPU it works, but it is slower, especially with the large models.

Is it a subscription?

No. You pay once, any amount from $3. There is no monthly fee and no account.

Why is the installer so big?

The speech models are inside it, so it works offline right after install. The main installer covers Hinglish Roman and the wake word. Install the optional extra models file only if you want the Indian English or Hinglish to English modes.

Why does Windows show a warning when I run it?

The installer is new and not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen may show "Windows protected your PC". Click "More info", then "Run anyway".

How is it different from Wispr Flow?

Wispr Flow turns your speech into text on its cloud servers, needs an account, and its free plan has a weekly word limit. WhisperWriter does it all on your own PC, works offline, and has no account and no word limit. Wispr Flow works on more devices and languages. See the full comparison.

Is there a Mac or Linux version?

Not right now. WhisperWriter is for Windows only.
